Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 2026

GhostWriter for Gmail ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your information.

Summary: We do not collect your personal data, we do not have a server to store your emails, and your API keys never leave your browser.

1. Data Storage & API Keys

  • Local Storage Only: All API keys (OpenAI, DeepSeek, Claude) provided by you are stored exclusively within your browser's chrome.storage.local.
  • No Developer Access: As the developer, I have no technical means to access your keys or your stored configurations.

2. Email Content & Sensitive Information

  • On-Demand Processing: The Extension only accesses the content of the Gmail compose window when you explicitly click the "Read from Gmail" button.
  • Direct Transmission: Your draft content is sent directly from your browser to the AI service provider you have configured (e.g., OpenAI API).
  • No Data Retention: We do not store, log, or monitor the text of your emails. Your data is not used by us to train any models.

3. Permissions (Sensitive Scopes)

  • Gmail Access: The Extension requires permission to interact with the Gmail interface to provide its core functionality: reading your current draft and inserting the AI-generated response.
  • Limited Use: We strictly adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Third-Party AI Providers

By using your own API Key, your data is subject to the privacy policy of the respective AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, or DeepSeek). We encourage you to review their terms.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the official Chrome Web Store support channel.
